Welcome to NextGen Billing Software!
This is a modern, high-speed Supermarket & Retail POS software equipped with
Smart AI Voice Billing in Tamil & English, GST Invoicing, Inventory Management,
and Thermal Receipt Printing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUICK START (HOW TO RUN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extract the "NextGen_Billing_App_v1.0" folder to your computer (e.g. C:\NextGen or Desktop).
2. Double-click "Launch_NextGen_Billing.vbs" (or "Start_NextGen_Billing.bat").
3. The software will open immediately, with the AI Voice engine active in the background.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEFAULT LOGIN CREDENTIALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Role: Admin
- Username: admin
- Password: admin
(You can also log in as Cashier: cashier / cashier123)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SMART VOICE BILLING COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the Cashier screen and click the "Voice Billing" microphone button.
Speak naturally in Tamil, English, or Thanglish:

A. Adding Products to Cart:
   - "Rendu aavin milk podu"          -> Adds 2 units of Aavin Milk
   - "Add 1 sun pure oil"             -> Adds 1 unit of Sun Pure Oil
   - "500 gram sugar kudu"            -> Adds 500g Sugar
   - "Moonu clinic plus shampoo"      -> Adds 3 Clinic Plus Shampoo sachets

B. Editing & Quantity Modifiers:
   - "Aavin milk qty 3 ah maathu"     -> Updates quantity to 3
   - "Remove oil"                     -> Deletes oil from current bill

C. Discounts & Finalizing:
   - "10 percent discount podu"       -> Applies 10% instant discount
   - "Cash payment"                   -> Selects Cash mode
   - "Print bill"                     -> Generates and prints thermal tax invoice

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KEY POS FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Product Management: Add, edit, barcoding, unit-value mapping, and 6 smart category tabs.
- Category Management: Create, edit, and reorganize supermarket aisles/departments.
- Customer Ledger: Track pending credit balances and customer phone numbers.
- Analytics & Reports: Daily/Monthly Sales, Stock alerts, GST Tax collections, and 1-Click Excel CSV Export.
- Database & Backup: 1-Click SQLite Database Backup to Desktop in Settings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

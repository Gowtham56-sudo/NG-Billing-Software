3. SMART VOICE BILLING COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the Cashier screen and click the "Voice Billing" microphone button.
Speak naturally in Tamil, English, or Thanglish to ADD items to the current
bill. Voice billing only adds items - use the on-screen cart to change a
quantity, remove an item, apply a discount, choose the payment method, or
print, exactly as you would for a barcode-scanned item.

A. Adding Products by Name and Quantity:
   - "Rendu tomato"                   -> Adds 2 kg of Tomato
   - "Sun pure oil 1 packet"          -> Adds 1 unit of Sun Pure Oil

B. Fractional & Weight Quantities:
   - "Vengayam arai kilo"             -> Adds 0.5 kg of Onion
   - "Chilli powder 50 gram"          -> Adds the matching pack size/weight

C. Budget-Based Quantities:
   - "20 roobaikku thakkali"          -> Adds however much Tomato Rs.20 buys

D. Multiple Items in One Sentence:
   - "Thakkali 2 kilo, vengayam 1 kilo" -> Adds both Tomato and Onion in one go

After speaking, review the items added to the cart on screen before finalizing
the bill - always double-check the voice-recognized items and quantities,
especially in a noisy shop environment.
